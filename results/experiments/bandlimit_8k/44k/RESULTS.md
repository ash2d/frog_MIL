# Results

Generated by `frog-report` from the runs of dataset `bae45880c5`. Do not edit by hand; rerun `frog run report`.

**Data:** 3647 hour-bags (44k-1clip 762, 44k-2clip 2885), 5-fold cross-validation over 3-day blocks. Every bag is scored once per seed by the fold model that never saw it (out-of-fold). Positives: G. chrysosticta 138/3647 (3.8%); O. berdemenos 480/3647 (13.2%). Chance AP = prevalence.  
**Metric:** average precision (AP) on the out-of-fold scores, averaged over seeds; macro = mean of the two species.  
**95% CI:** block bootstrap (whole 3-day blocks, resampled within each recording regime), B=2000, same resamples for every model (paired). `*` = difference whose CI excludes 0.  
**Reference model (◆):** `mlp256-ord0.5-s2-bl8k/lme`, the best mean *validation* macro AP. The best on test is `mlp256-ord0.5-s2-bl8k/lme`; it is not used for selection.  
**Seed SD:** spread of macro AP across training seeds.  
**Baselines** (no audio training): *clock* = training-fold presence rate for the bag's (hour of day × month); *Perch zero-shot* = max over the bag of Perch v2 logits for congeneric species or its 'Frog' class.

Model IDs are `{probe}-{target}-s2/{pooling}`: `linear`/`mlp256` = probe, `bin`/`ord0.5` = binary or ordinal loss (weight 0.5), `s2` = contiguous 5 s windows. Weights and predictions are in the run store, `runs/bae45880c5/<model_id>/`.
